The booster is falling at 140 m/s toward a barge at sea. Its engine can't throttle below 40%, so it can't hover. Light it too early and the fuel runs out. Too late and it hits the deck.

  • Space Engine on or off
  • ←→ Lean the booster
  • ↑ Slow down ↓ Speed up. Let go to hold your speed.
  • R Restart

Steering moves you sideways only while the engine is burning. With it off, the arrows just tilt the booster.

You don't fly this time. The flight computer does, using the nine settings below. Change them, press Run flight, and watch what happens.
